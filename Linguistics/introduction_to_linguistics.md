## Intro
#### Date: 17 November 2026
- Teach of properties of language, phonetics, phonology, syntax, semantics, morphology, pragmatics\
- Scope of Linguistics (from inside to outside)**
  - Phonetics: speech and sounds
  - Phonology: phonemes
  - Morphology: words
  - Syntax: phrases and sentences
  - Semantics: literal meaning
  - Pragmatics: contextual meaning
- Properties of language
  - **creativity** - new words and sentences that we have never heard of before keep getting produced and understood
  - **generality** - languages have grammars for sounds, words and sentences
    - parts of a syllable: onset and rhyme
    - the rhyme has a nucleus and coda
  - **parity** - languages are all equal. There are no primitive languages.
  - **universality** - features that all languages share. e.g. all languages have at least 3 vowels, they all have nouns and verbs
  - **mutability** - languages change overtime
 
- **Descriptivist** - language is described as it occurs
- **Prescriptivist** - Tell people what to say and how to say it
